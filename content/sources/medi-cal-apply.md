## What Medi-Cal is

Medi-Cal is California's free or low-cost health insurance for people with low income. It is California's version of the federal Medicaid program. It pays for doctor visits, hospital care, prescriptions, and other health services.

## How to apply for Medi-Cal

You can apply for Medi-Cal at any time of year. There is no enrollment deadline.

- Online: BenefitsCal.com (the state's benefits website) or CoveredCA.com.
- By phone: call your county human services office. San Mateo County Human Services Agency: 1-800-223-8383 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.). Santa Clara County Social Services Agency: 1-877-962-3633, or the Benefits Assistance Center at (408) 758-3800.
- Covered California can also help you apply: 1-800-300-1506.
- In person: visit your county human services office.

The county decides if you qualify. If you need more information, the county will send you a letter asking for proof, such as pay stubs.

## Medi-Cal income limits for 2026

Medi-Cal uses the Federal Poverty Level (FPL). For 2026, 100% FPL is $15,960 per year for one person, plus $5,680 for each additional person in the household.

- Adults ages 19 to 64: up to 138% FPL.
- Children under 19: up to 266% FPL.
- Pregnant people: up to 213% FPL for full-scope, no-cost Medi-Cal. A pregnant person counts as two (or more, for twins) household members.
- Pregnant people with income above 213% and up to 322% FPL may qualify for the Medi-Cal Access Program (MCAP).
- In San Mateo and Santa Clara counties, children with income above 266% and up to 322% FPL may qualify for the County Children's Health Initiative Program (C-CHIP).

People with income above the Medi-Cal limit may qualify for a Covered California health plan with financial help.

## Asset limits for some people (2026)

Most people applying for Medi-Cal do not have an asset (property) test. An asset limit applies to people who are 65 or older, have a disability, live in a nursing home, or are in a family whose income is too high under the federal tax rules. Through June 30, 2027, the asset limit is $130,000 for one person plus $65,000 for each additional household member (up to 10 people). Your home, one car, household items, and some retirement accounts do not count. Starting July 1, 2027, the limit will be $21,000 for one person and $31,000 for two people.

## Children and pregnant people

Children under 19 and pregnant people (during pregnancy and for one year after) can get full-scope Medi-Cal regardless of immigration status, if they meet the income rules. Some adults cannot newly enroll in full-scope Medi-Cal since January 1, 2026 because of immigration status; see "Medi-Cal changes in 2026 and 2027."

## Help for pregnant people

A pregnant person with income at or below 213% FPL can get immediate temporary coverage for prenatal care through Presumptive Eligibility while their Medi-Cal application is processed. For information, call (800) 824-0088.
