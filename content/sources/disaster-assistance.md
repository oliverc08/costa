## FEMA disaster assistance

After a disaster that the President declares for Individual Assistance, FEMA may give money and services to people affected. You can apply:
- Online at DisasterAssistance.gov.
- With the FEMA App.
- By phone: FEMA helpline 1-800-621-3362, open seven days a week. Help is available in most languages.
- In person at a Disaster Recovery Center (find one at fema.gov/drc).

## Immigration status and FEMA

To get FEMA cash assistance, you or a member of your household must be a U.S. citizen, non-citizen national, or qualified non-citizen (for example, a green card holder, refugee, or asylee).

Households with mixed immigration status only need one member who qualifies, including a minor child under 18, to apply. A parent or guardian who does not qualify can apply as co-applicant on behalf of a qualifying minor child who lives with them, using the child's Social Security number. The parent or guardian does not have to give any information about their own immigration status. FEMA may ask for the child's Social Security card and U.S. birth certificate.

People who do not qualify for FEMA cash assistance may still get short-term, non-cash emergency help.

## CalFresh food replacement after a disaster or power outage

If food you bought with CalFresh was destroyed or spoiled because of a disaster, fire, flood, or power outage, you can ask for replacement benefits.
- Contact your county: call 1-877-847-3663 (1-877-847-FOOD).
- Fill out, sign, and turn in form CF 303. Include your contact information, what happened, and the date and time of the outage.
- Normally you must ask within 10 days of the food loss. After big disasters, the state sometimes extends this, often to 30 days.

## Disaster CalFresh (D-CalFresh)

After some major disasters, Disaster CalFresh (D-SNAP) gives one month of food benefits to affected people who normally would not get CalFresh. It is only available when the federal government approves it for a specific disaster. Check the California Department of Social Services Disaster Help Center.

## Local disaster information

Dial 2-1-1 for local disaster shelters, food, and recovery resources in San Mateo and Santa Clara counties.
