## What CalEITC is

The California Earned Income Tax Credit (CalEITC) is cash back for low-income working Californians. You must file a California state tax return to get it. You can get money back even if you owe no tax.

## Who can get CalEITC (tax year 2025)

You must meet all of these during the tax year:
- Be at least 18 years old, or have a qualifying child.
- Have earned income (such as wages or self-employment) of at least $1 and not more than $32,900.
- Have a valid Social Security number or ITIN for yourself, your spouse, and any qualifying children.
- Live in California for more than half the year.
- Not be claimed as a dependent by someone else (unless you have a qualifying child).

## How much CalEITC can be (tax year 2025)

Maximum amounts (most people get less, based on income):
- No qualifying children: up to $302.
- 1 qualifying child: up to $2,016.
- 2 qualifying children: up to $3,339.
- 3 or more qualifying children: up to $3,756.

## Young Child Tax Credit (YCTC)

If you qualify for CalEITC and have a qualifying child under 6 at the end of the tax year, you may also get the Young Child Tax Credit: up to $1,189 per tax return for tax year 2025. The credit gets smaller when earned income is above $27,425 and ends at $32,901. Some families with $0 earned income can still qualify for YCTC.

## Filing with an ITIN

An ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is a tax number the IRS gives to people who cannot get a Social Security number. Californians can use an ITIN to file a state tax return and claim three refundable credits: CalEITC, the Young Child Tax Credit, and the Foster Youth Tax Credit. Claim them on Form FTB 3514.

To get or renew an ITIN, apply to the IRS using Form W-7. The IRS issues and renews ITINs.

The federal Earned Income Tax Credit (federal EITC) is different: it requires a Social Security number, so ITIN filers can claim the California credits but not the federal EITC.

## Free help filing taxes

The IRS Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) program offers free tax help for people with low to moderate income. Find a site at irs.gov/vita or call 1-800-906-9887. The Franchise Tax Board's CalEITC calculator can estimate your credit.
