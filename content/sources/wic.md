## What WIC is

WIC (Women, Infants, and Children) gives healthy food, nutrition education, and breastfeeding support to pregnant people, new parents, babies, and young children. WIC benefits are loaded on a card to buy specific healthy foods.

## Who can get WIC

You must live in California and be in one of these groups:
- Pregnant.
- Breastfeeding a baby under 12 months old.
- Had a baby or a pregnancy ended within the last 6 months.
- An infant or a child under 5 years old (including foster children).

Fathers, grandparents, foster parents, and other guardians can apply for WIC for a child under 5 they care for.

## WIC income limits (May 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027)

Gross (before taxes) monthly household income must be at or below:
- 1 person: $2,461
- 2 people: $3,337
- 3 people: $4,212
- 4 people: $5,088
- 5 people: $5,964
- 6 people: $6,839
- 7 people: $7,715
- 8 people: $8,591
- Each additional person: add $876

An unborn baby can be counted in the household size.

## Automatic income eligibility

If you or your child already get Medi-Cal, CalFresh, or CalWORKs, you automatically meet the WIC income rule. You still need to be in a WIC group (for example, pregnant or a child under 5), and WIC staff will do a short nutrition check.

## Immigration status and WIC

WIC does not require a Social Security number or proof of citizenship. Families can get WIC no matter their immigration status, as long as they live in California and meet the program rules.

## How to apply for WIC

Call your local WIC office or California WIC at 1-800-852-5770 (toll-free) to make an appointment. You can also check if you may qualify at myfamily.wic.ca.gov. Only WIC staff can officially confirm that you qualify. Bring proof of identity, proof of address, and proof of income (or your Medi-Cal, CalFresh, or CalWORKs card or letter).
