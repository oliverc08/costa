## What CalFresh is

CalFresh gives monthly money for food on an EBT card (like a debit card). It can be used to buy most foods at most grocery stores and farmers markets. It is California's version of the federal SNAP program.

## How to apply for CalFresh

- Online at BenefitsCal.com.
- By phone: call 1-877-847-3663 (1-877-847-FOOD) to connect to your county office. San Mateo County: 1-800-223-8383. Santa Clara County: 1-877-962-3633.
- In person at your county human services office.

After you apply, the county will do an interview, usually by phone, and ask for proof of income.

## CalFresh income limits (October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026)

Most households can qualify if their gross (before taxes) monthly income is at or below these amounts (200% of the Federal Poverty Level):

- 1 person: $2,610
- 2 people: $3,526
- 3 people: $4,442
- 4 people: $5,360
- 5 people: $6,276
- 6 people: $7,192
- 7 people: $8,110
- 8 people: $9,026
- Each additional person: add $918

Households where an older adult or a person with a disability is a separate household have different rules. The county decides eligibility and the benefit amount.

## Maximum monthly CalFresh amounts (October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026)

- 1 person: $298
- 2 people: $546
- 3 people: $785
- 4 people: $994
- 5 people: $1,183
- 6 people: $1,421
- 7 people: $1,571
- 8 people: $1,789
- Each additional person: add $218

Most households get less than the maximum. The amount depends on income and expenses like rent and utilities.

## CalFresh changes from the federal H.R. 1 law

- Since November 1, 2025: households without a member over 60 or with a disability must have heating or cooling costs separate from their rent to claim a utility deduction. Some households got less money because of this.
- Since April 1, 2026: many lawfully present immigrants no longer qualify for CalFresh.
- Since June 1, 2026: more CalFresh recipients may need to participate in work or community engagement activities to keep their benefits.

If you are not sure how these changes affect you, contact your county.

## Keeping CalFresh

Most CalFresh households must send a periodic report (form SAR 7) every six months and renew once a year. Return forms by the due date or your benefits may stop.

## If you disagree with a decision

You have 90 days from the date of the Notice of Action to ask for a State Hearing. Call (800) 743-8525 or use the form on the back of the notice.
