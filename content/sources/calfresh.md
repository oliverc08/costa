## What CalFresh is

CalFresh gives monthly money for food on an EBT card (like a debit card). It can be used to buy most foods at most grocery stores and farmers markets. It is California's version of the federal SNAP program.

## How to apply for CalFresh

- Online at BenefitsCal.com.
- By phone: call 1-877-847-3663 (1-877-847-FOOD) to connect to your county office. San Mateo County: 1-800-223-8383. Santa Clara County: 1-877-962-3633.
- In person at your county human services office.

After you apply, the county will do an interview, usually by phone, and ask for proof of income.

## CalFresh income limits (October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027)

These are the gross (before taxes) monthly limits at 200% of the Federal Poverty Level for households under California's Modified Categorical Eligibility (MCE) rules:

- 1 person: $2,660
- 2 people: $3,608
- 3 people: $4,554
- 4 people: $5,500
- 5 people: $6,448
- 6 people: $7,394
- 7 people: $8,340
- 8 people: $9,288
- Each additional person: add $948

Households not covered by MCE generally use a 130% gross-income limit and a 100% net-income limit. Households with an older adult or a person with a disability can also have different rules. These figures are only a screening guide: the county decides eligibility and the benefit amount. See the [current CDSS income insert](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/portals/9/calfreshoutreach/220000_English_Income_Inserts_FFY2027.pdf) and [Santa Clara County's detailed eligibility chart](https://stgenssa.sccgov.org/debs/program_handbooks/charts/assets/2CalFresh/CFMonthAllIncomeElig.htm).

## Maximum monthly CalFresh amounts (October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027)

- 1 person: $306
- 2 people: $562
- 3 people: $808
- 4 people: $1,023
- 5 people: $1,217
- 6 people: $1,463
- 7 people: $1,616
- 8 people: $1,841
- Each additional person: add $225

Most households get less than the maximum. The amount depends on income and expenses like rent and utilities.

## CalFresh changes from the federal H.R. 1 law

- Since November 1, 2025: some households can no longer use a heating or cooling utility deduction unless they pay that cost separately from rent. This change depends on household circumstances; ask the county how it affects your case.
- Since April 1, 2026: some immigration categories that were previously eligible for CalFresh are no longer eligible under federal rules. Other household members may still be eligible, and the California Food Assistance Program may be an option for some people. See the [CDSS H.R. 1 FAQ](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/benefits-services/food-nutrition-services/calfresh/frequently-asked-questions) or ask the county.
- Since June 1, 2026: some CalFresh recipients ages 18–64 may need to meet work or community engagement rules to get or keep benefits. Exemptions and county waivers apply. See the [CDSS work rules page](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/calfresh/abawd) or ask the county whether the rule applies.

If you are not sure how these changes affect you, contact your county.

## Keeping CalFresh

Most CalFresh households must send a periodic report (form SAR 7) every six months and renew once a year. Return forms by the due date or your benefits may stop.

## If you disagree with a decision

You have 90 days from the date of the Notice of Action to ask for a State Hearing. Call (800) 743-8525 or use the form on the back of the notice.
