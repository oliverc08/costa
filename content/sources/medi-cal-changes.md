## You must report changes within 10 days

You must tell your county human services office within 10 days when something in your life changes that could affect your Medi-Cal. You can report online at BenefitsCal.com, by phone, by email or fax, or in person.

Changes you must report include:
- You move or change your mailing address.
- Your income or job changes.
- Someone moves into or out of your home, or you have or adopt a baby.
- You become pregnant.
- You get married, divorced, or separated.
- You get other health insurance, like through a job or Medicare.
- Your disability status changes.
- Your tax filing status or tax dependents change.
- You change your name, date of birth, or Social Security number.

## If you move to another county in California

Your Medi-Cal case can move with you. This is called an Inter-County Transfer. Report your new address to either your old county or your new county within 10 days. Your Medi-Cal health plan in your old county ends on the last day of the month, and you will need to choose a health plan in your new county.

## If your income goes up or down

Report income changes within 10 days. If your income goes up, you may move to a different Medi-Cal program, or you may qualify for a Covered California plan with financial help instead. If your income goes down, you may qualify for more help. The county will send you a notice explaining any change.

## Why reporting matters

The county uses your address to mail renewal forms and notices. If your address is wrong, you could miss your renewal and lose your Medi-Cal.
